# Research methods expansion: editorial and visual notes

This pack adds 30 research, evidence, strategy, and content terms to the existing glossary. It contains original Korean explanations and original fictional teaching scenes. It does not import, summarize by page, or claim to have newly reviewed the user’s ten PDFs. Existing PDF attribution remains separate.

## Reading and attribution

The sources below were inspected through the web retrieval tool. The canonical source pages were opened; Material 2 Writing used the primary page’s indexed body because opening the JavaScript page exposed only its shell. No Mobbin screenshot, product screenshot, remote image, or third-party diagram is reproduced. One primary or author-maintained page is assigned to each term to distribute the source reuse budget. The explanatory scenes and all product names, records, quotes, observations, and numbers are original fictional teaching material, not claims drawn from the source.

## Boundaries that the content preserves

- Card sorting explores people’s grouping criteria; tree testing evaluates navigation hierarchy; first-click testing does not prove task completion.
- Heuristic evaluation and cognitive walkthrough are expert review methods. Their findings are not presented as observed user behavior.
- Moderated and unmoderated tests differ in facilitation, not automatically in rigor. Thinking aloud is a protocol and can influence natural timing.
- Diary and interview records are self-reports. Contextual observation includes the work environment and does not justify generalizing a single observed case.
- A/B testing requires suitable randomization, measurement, sample design, and analysis. No invented winning variant or guaranteed causal conclusion is given.
- Benchmark values are explicitly fictional. Between-period differences alone do not establish causality.
- Sampling, selection, and survivorship bias are related but distinct concepts. Each scene names the selection or exclusion mechanism.
- Confidence intervals use the frequentist repeated-coverage interpretation. They do not assign probability to a fixed parameter inside one realized interval. The three illustrative intervals do not pretend to reproduce a 95% simulation.
- Correlation is shown as association with a plausible common-cause explanation; arrows do not claim that help views cause errors.
- Opportunity solution trees connect outcomes, research-backed opportunities, solution candidates, and assumption tests. The fictional opportunities are marked as requiring research.
- A service blueprint includes the customer, visible touchpoints, internal actions, and support systems; it is not only a customer journey.
- MVP scope follows a testable learning need. Reduced scope does not permit neglecting usability or privacy.
- Microcopy is treated as task-supporting interface language. It is not conflated with NN/g’s different concept of standalone microcontent.

## Visual and implementation decisions

`ResearchExamples.tsx` contains an explicit scene for every research-pack ID. Appropriate visual artifacts replace forced interaction: grouping boards, navigation trees, session records, annotated fictional interfaces, task results, experiment plans, service lanes, an opportunity tree, an assumptions matrix, a repository record, source comparisons, a conceptual scatterplot, and confidence intervals. The visuals are HTML/CSS and one inline SVG; all remain editable in source.

Every scene visibly says “교육용 가상 예시” and “정적 자료”. Decorative controls are spans, not buttons, inputs, links, or disabled controls. No scene claims to record, submit, run a study, or perform a live statistical calculation. They therefore add no artificial keyboard stops.

The component uses a container query at 480px. Two- and three-column layouts become one column; task-success participants wrap into two rows; blueprint lanes stack inside each stage. The four-quadrant assumptions matrix remains a true 2×2 relation with concise Korean labels. All classes are scoped with `xe-research-`. The restrained green accent is supplemented by text, borders, icons, or labels.

## Checks and remaining scope

- All 30 manifest IDs are present exactly once; every source reference resolves; every term has a visual switch case; no self-referential confused-with links.
- Prettier and ESLint were run on the owned files. Project-wide TypeScript and browser checks are handled with integration by the root agent.
- Source verification does not imply that every method should be used on every project. The application and critique sections give method-specific limitations and the evidence needed next.
- No empirical claims are made from the fictional scenes. The small success-rate and benchmarking samples demonstrate denominators and comparability, not statistical power.
- Source pages and protocols may evolve; current official standards or exact statistical procedures should be consulted for a real research design.
