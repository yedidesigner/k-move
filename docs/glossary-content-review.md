## Corrections with checked sources

- **Accessibility; Alternative text:** Accessibility includes perceiving, understanding and operating; alt text conveys purpose/meaning, and decorative images should not be needlessly announced. W3C WAI: https://www.w3.org/WAI/fundamentals/accessibility-intro/ and https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/
- **Aggregated empathy maps; Empathy map:** Aggregate within a relevant user segment; distinguish research evidence from assumptions. Nielsen Norman Group: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/empathy-mapping/
- **Asymmetrical layout:** Asymmetry can produce visual balance; it is not synonymous with imbalance. Nielsen Norman Group: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/principles-visual-design/
- **Corporation:** Changed misleading translation “대기업” to “법인 기업”; organizational scale is not the defining property. California Secretary of State: https://www.sos.ca.gov/business-programs/business-entities/starting-business/types
- **Design Sprint:** GV's five-day format is a specific established approach; other durations exist. Removed universal “8 hours × 5 days” implication. https://www.gv.com/sprint and https://designsprintkit.withgoogle.com/planning
- **Design thinking:** Five familiar modes are not an obligatory linear sequence. Stanford d.school: https://dschool.stanford.edu/tools/design-thinking-bootleg
- **Dopamine:** Replaced simplistic pleasure-only definition with reward learning, motivation and movement; the original learning example does not claim an app feature causes a measured dopamine response. NIMH: https://www.nimh.nih.gov/research/research-funded-by-nimh/rdoc/units/molecules/149896 ; NIH: https://beta.nih.gov/news-events/nih-research-matters/treating-addiction
- **F-shape layout:** F-shaped scanning is conditional rather than a fixed rule for every interface. Original researcher explanation: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/f-shaped-pattern-reading-web-content/
- **Font:** Clarified the actual character set/file versus typography settings. Font foundry glossary: https://support.monotype.com/en/articles/8949273-glossary ; Letterform Archive: https://letterformarchive.org/fonts-and-typefaces/

## Other editorial clarifications

- **Conversion rate** specifies that the denominator and observation period must be defined. The 20/200 = 10% example is illustrative arithmetic, not an external benchmark.

## Editorial/conceptual corrections

- `Research report`: removed the unsupported assertion that research reports contain fewer visuals than presentation decks.
- `Responsive web app`: applies to browser-based applications across screen sizes, not only mobile browsers.
- `Recency bias`: distinguish disproportionate weight in judgment from simply remembering recent items.
- `Scope creep`: can arise during execution, not only planning; specify expansion without appropriate time/resource adjustment.
- `Scope of work`: Korean label changed from “업무 범위서” to “업무 범위”, distinguishing the scope itself from a document that records it.
- `Second round of interviews`: remove an unsupported “usually face to face” claim.
- `Statement of Work`: define its content without a blanket claim of legal enforceability.
- `Similarity`: related grouping is the core perceptual principle; similar appearance does not prove identical function.
- `Sunk cost fallacy`: specify irrecoverable past investment influencing future choices.
- `Time on task` and `User error rates`: state that measurement definitions must be consistent; an error does not establish user blame.
- `User experience`: include anticipated use, not only the instant of direct interaction.
- `Vulnerable populations`: describe situational risks and protections, not inherent inability of a whole population to consent.
- `Visual balance`: allow balanced asymmetric arrangements instead of equating balance with left/right symmetry.
- `Z-shape layout`: mark it as a compositional approach; actual attention varies with content and reading direction.
- `WCAG` Korean label becomes “웹 콘텐츠 접근성 지침(약칭)”; `WebAIM` becomes “웹 접근성 전문 조직”.

Examples are newly written fictional scenarios for this glossary. No source examples or survey items have been reproduced verbatim.
